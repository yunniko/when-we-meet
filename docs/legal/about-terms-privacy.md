## About

When We Meet is a free tool for finding a time that works for a group. A
creator picks a date range and a daily time window and gets a link; anyone
with that link marks the times they're free (and, optionally, which of
those they'd prefer); everyone with the link sees the combined result.
There is no account, no email, and no signup for anyone — creator or
participant — at any point.

## Privacy — how your data is used

### What we collect

- **The display name you type** when you join a room. It is not verified
  in any way — anyone with the room's link can type any name, including
  someone else's.
- **The room's title and description**, if its creator chose to add them.
- **The date range, daily time window, and the availability marks** (can /
  can't / prefer) you paint on the grid.
- **Two small cookies per room you join**: one that remembers your
  identity within that specific room (so you don't have to retype your
  name on return visits), and — only if you created the room — one that
  lets you pick or clear the final meeting time. Both are readable only by
  our server (`httpOnly`), scoped to that one room, and set to expire
  after 180 days — though in practice they almost always outlive the room
  itself, which is deleted much sooner (see "How long we keep it," below).
- **One cookie for your interface language** (English/Russian/Czech/
  German), kept for up to a year. It contains nothing but a language code.
- **Standard web server request logs** (IP address, requested page,
  timestamp), kept only as long as needed for security and abuse
  investigation — never used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

### What we don't do

- No accounts, no passwords, no email collection, ever.
- No analytics, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts of any
  kind loaded into any page.
- We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

### How long we keep it

Every room — and every name, description, and availability mark in it —
is **automatically and permanently deleted 3 days** after its planning
range ends, or 3 days after a final meeting time is picked, whichever
applies. This runs on its own; there is nothing to request. You can also
remove your own name and marks from a room immediately, at any time,
using the "Leave room" link inside it, without waiting for that
expiry.

### Your rights

Because there's no account and no identity that outlives a room, most
data-subject requests are already self-service: use "Leave room" to erase
your data on the spot, or simply wait — everything is gone within days
regardless of what you do. For anything this doesn't cover, contact
info@julienika.cz.
